# Product on a person

Build a frame in which the product stays recognizable, while the person, the pose and the setting
serve the user's idea.

## Product accuracy

- Carry over the meaningful traits of the source product: cut, silhouette, color, material,
  print, logo, lettering and hardware.
- Name explicitly the detail that matters to show, and choose an angle where it reads.
- For small elements, use a separate close-up when the chosen generation model does not hold
  them in a wider shot.

## Frame and pose

Choose the shot size and the pose for the request and for the product's visibility: a wide shot
for the silhouette, a tighter frame for an accessory or for how the product is used, a close-up
for material and details. Follow the user's scene and mood; when the brief is incomplete, choose
a consistent solution from the context.
