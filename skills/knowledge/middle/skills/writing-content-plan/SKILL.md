# Content plan

Build the plan in the form the user asked for: a list of directions, a set of recurring sections,
a calendar, a publication series or a combination of these.

## Basis

Take into account the goal, the platform, the period, the audience, the available materials and
the realistic publishing frequency. Ask only about a decision that would noticeably change the
plan; infer the rest from context and briefly state the assumptions you made.

## Content

- Choose recurring sections that serve the goal and yield enough distinct topics.
- Alternate formats and content purposes in a proportion that suits the project.
- Formulate concrete topics, and the next step when a publication needs one.
- For a calendar, give the date or day, the topic, the format and the goal of each publication;
  add other columns only when the task needs them.

Tie numeric forecasts of results to a measurable baseline that has been provided. The plan itself
fixes topics, formats and rhythm, not a promise of metrics.
