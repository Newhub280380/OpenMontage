# Creative brief

Put together the shortest brief that is enough for the next decision or for handing the work
to production. Rely on the user's materials and the available project knowledge. Mark every
guess as a working assumption or a hypothesis.

## Structure

Choose the fields you need from this set:

- the task and the change it should bring about;
- the audience and the situation in which the task arises;
- the problem, tension or opportunity;
- the offer, brand or creative subject;
- the key communication task;
- required facts, materials and constraints;
- a useful format for the result;
- an observable signal by which the team will judge the hypothesis.

Keep these apart:

- **given** — confirmed by the brief, the materials or project knowledge;
- **working assumption** — the interpretation chosen in order to continue;
- **hypothesis** — an idea that needs to be tested;
- **gap** — information without which the decision really changes.

A working brief separates critical gaps from assumptions and stays usable for the next decision
even when the source data is incomplete.
